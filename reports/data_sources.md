## Traceability limitations

The URLs stored in the CSV point to source results/category pages, not unique individual adverts. The records were manually transcribed from visible results and cannot all be independently verified at advert level from the saved data. Listings may change or disappear after collection.

Before using this dataset for a business decision, revisit the source pages, capture a direct advert URL for each observation where available, record a verification timestamp, and review possible cross-platform duplicates. No seller contact details or personal information were intentionally collected.

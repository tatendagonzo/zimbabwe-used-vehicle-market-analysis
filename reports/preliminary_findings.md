The mean is higher than the median, partly because the sample includes a US$50,000 Toyota Fortuner. These statistics describe only this small convenience sample.

## Composition

Toyota Axio observations account for 18 of 21 records. The remaining observations are one Honda Fit, one Nissan X-Trail and one Toyota Fortuner. This concentration limits comparisons between vehicle models.

## Data-quality flags

- ZWV006 and ZWV016 have matching make, model, year, price and mileage (Toyota Axio, 2018, US$15,000, 71,000 km) but come from different websites. They may represent the same advert, but the available result-page URLs do not prove that. Both are retained and flagged for manual review.
- Three observations have no transmission value and no fuel-type value in the captured result.
- Source links are results/category pages rather than individual advert URLs.

## Interpretation and next steps

These statistics are not estimates of Zimbabwe-wide market prices. Asking prices are not confirmed sale prices. The sample is too small and too heavily weighted toward Toyota Axio listings for reliable cross-model comparisons or causal conclusions.

Next steps: capture individual advert URLs and verification timestamps; review the possible duplicate pair; expand the sample across makes, models, locations and price bands; standardise duty status and negotiability; and repeat collection over time.
